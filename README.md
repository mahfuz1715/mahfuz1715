## Featured Research

### CaneScan-XAI
**Explainable Ensemble Deep Learning for Sugarcane Leaf Disease Classification**

An explainable deep-learning framework combining pretrained CNNs, SE/CBAM attention mechanisms, weighted soft voting, and Grad-CAM for 14-class sugarcane leaf disease recognition.

- Final ensemble accuracy: **97.20%**
- F1-score: **97.22%**
- Accepted and presented at **IEEE RAAICON 2026**


[View Repository](https://github.com/mahfuz1715/CaneScan-XAI)

---

### WheatHead-26M
**Cross-Domain Wheat Head Detection and Counting on GWHD 2021**

A comparative study of YOLOv8 and YOLO26 for wheat-head detection, cross-domain generalization, computational efficiency, and image-level counting.

- F1-score: **0.763**
- mAP@0.50: **0.762**
- Counting MAE: **8.085**
- Accepted for presentation at **IEEE WIECON-ECE 2026**


[View Repository](https://github.com/mahfuz1715/WheatHead-26M)

---

### LumaRep
**Interpretable Lumbar Spinal MRI Classification via Self-Supervised Learning**

A medical imaging study exploring supervised, self-supervised, and semi-supervised learning using BYOL, DINO, and STAC, together with Grad-CAM and occlusion sensitivity for interpretability.

- BYOL-VGG16 accuracy: **95.63%**
- F1-score: **95.62%**
- Accepted for oral presentation as a regular paper at **IEEE CSNT 2027**


[View Repository](https://github.com/mahfuz1715/LumaRep)
